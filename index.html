Zhejiang Xingba Industry & Trade Co., Ltd.

浙江星霸工贸有限公司

Established in Dongyang City, Zhejiang Province, China, Xingba specializes in manufacturing colored wooden frame writing boards — A-frame menu boards, stand boards, table boards, felt letter boards and wall-mounted gallery boards.

At present, our major markets are distributed in the USA as one of the best wooden board sellers on Amazon. We are also establishing strategic cooperation with chain markets in EU countries.

Our vision is to be the most professional manufacturer of wooden frame boards in the world, bringing high quality to life as well as classic wood culture & art for leisure.
